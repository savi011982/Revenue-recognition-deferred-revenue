# Revenue Recognition & Deferred Revenue

> **Portfolio case study | Fictional business data | Senior Finance & Accounting**

## Business context

**Business:** Fictional SaaS & Services Portfolio  
**Role demonstrated:** Revenue Accountant / Senior Accountant

A SaaS revenue accounting simulation covering contract schedules, monthly recognition, deferred revenue and journal-entry support.

## Business problem

The objective was to take accounting data and turn it into a controlled, reviewable finance process with clear supporting schedules and exceptions.

## Approach

1. Reviewed source accounting data and supporting schedules.
2. Reconciled relevant balances and investigated differences.
3. Separated timing differences from accounting exceptions.
4. Prepared supporting schedules and proposed accounting actions.
5. Produced a management-ready output.
6. Documented assumptions and unresolved items rather than forcing unexplained balances to zero.

## Key findings

- Contract values were mapped into monthly recognition schedules.
- Deferred revenue is rolled forward after monthly recognition.
- Contract value is reconciled to recognized revenue plus remaining deferred revenue.
- Sample journal-entry support was prepared for selected periods.
- The model uses simplified assumptions and is intended as a portfolio demonstration.

## Portfolio disclosure

This is a **fictional portfolio simulation** created to demonstrate accounting workflow and analytical capability. Company names, customers, transactions, balances and other data are not client information.

Where accounting treatment depends on contract terms or applicable standards, the project states its assumptions rather than presenting a simplified portfolio model as a universal accounting conclusion.
